# LĀEL identity

The name is always LĀEL, retaining the macron over A. Do not publish the private origin of the name, add a registration symbol, or invent dates or business history.

## Typography and wordmark

Manrope Variable provides the display voice, with DM Sans Variable for body text and controls. Both are self-hosted through Fontsource packages under the SIL Open Font License; copies are in `docs/licenses/`.

`public/brand/wordmark.svg` contains original geometric path lettering. The inline React version is in `src/components/wordmark.tsx`. The macron is a deliberate separate horizontal stroke; do not replace it with an accent or remove it. Keep clear space around the mark and preserve its aspect ratio. The favicon is an original simplified Ā monogram.

## Imagery

The five WebP images in `public/images/` were created with the built-in image generation tool specifically for this sample storefront. They are concept visuals, not photographs of verified LĀEL garments or evidence of an actual campaign. Original generated PNG files are retained outside the repository; the website uses optimized WebP assets committed to the repository. `docs/image-prompts.md` records the generation prompts. All demo product information must be replaced or confirmed before sales.

## Voice

Quiet, clear, and intentional. Speak of design aspirations honestly. Avoid invented heritage, certifications, testimonials, exaggerated material claims, and implied operations for future concepts.
